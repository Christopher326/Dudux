Our Services

At Dudux Cleaning Company, we provide professional cleaning solutions for homes, businesses, and commercial properties throughout the greater Johannesburg area. Whether you need a one-time deep clean or regular maintenance, our experienced team is committed to delivering exceptional results.

Residential Cleaning

Keep your home fresh, clean, and healthy with our comprehensive residential cleaning services. We clean bedrooms, kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, and more with attention to every detail.

Commercial & Office Cleaning

We provide reliable cleaning services for offices, retail stores, and commercial spaces, helping you maintain a clean, professional environment for employees and customers.

Deep Cleaning

Our deep cleaning service targets built-up dirt, grease, grime, and hard-to-reach areas, leaving your property thoroughly cleaned and refreshed.

Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning

Whether you're moving into a new property or leaving one behind, we'll ensure the space is spotless and ready for its next occupants.

Post-Construction Cleaning

After renovations or construction work, we remove dust, debris, and residue to make your property clean, safe, and ready for use.

Kitchen Cleaning

We thoroughly clean countertops, cupboards, sinks, stovetops, ovens, appliances, and other kitchen surfaces to maintain a hygienic cooking environment.

Bathroom Cleaning

Our team disinfects toilets, showers, bathtubs, sinks, mirrors, and tiles, ensuring your bathroom is clean, fresh, and germ-free.

Window Cleaning

We clean interior and exterior windows, removing dust, smudges, and streaks to leave your glass sparkling.

Floor Cleaning

We vacuum, sweep, mop, and polish various floor surfaces to restore their shine and cleanliness.

Upholstery & Furniture Cleaning

We carefully clean sofas, chairs, and other upholstered furniture to remove dust, stains, and everyday dirt.

Regular Cleaning Plans

Choose from weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly cleaning services tailored to your schedule and cleaning needs.

Custom Cleaning Services

Every property is different. We offer customised cleaning solutions designed to meet your specific requirements.
